A LETTER FROM YOUR MEDHAK

Dear Sakshu ❤️

✦

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki humari story kisi planned story jaisi nahi thi.

Hum bachpan mein DPS mein mile, phir waqt ne humein alag-alag raaston par bhej diya.

Aur phir 2022 mein ek random si mulaqat ne humein dobara ek dusre ke saamne la khada kiya.

Uske baad Instagram ki woh request, aapka pehle meri request delete kar dena aur phir khud request bhejna 😂❤️...

Pata nahi tab hum dono ko kya pata tha ki wahi se itni beautiful story shuru hone wali hai.

Baatein hoti gayi, dosti badhti gayi, aur phir bina kisi ek din ke, bina kisi ek moment ke, hum dono ko naturally ek dusre se feelings ho gayi.

Aaj jab main humari chhoti-chhoti memories ke baare mein sochta hoon— coaching ke raste mein milna, birthdays par sabse pehle ek dusre ko wish karna, aur woh pera jo aap Bol Bam se mere liye lekar aayi thi— tab realize hota hai ki pyaar shayad inhi chhoti-chhoti cheezon se banta hai.

Sakshu, aapke saath mujhe ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai.

Aapke saath mujhe ghar jaisa feel hota hai. ❤️

Duniya mein kitni bhi bheed ho, kitni bhi problems ho, kitna bhi difficult din ho— aapse baat karna ya aapke paas hona mere liye duniya ka sabse zyada sukoon hai.

Main nahi jaanta future humare liye kya-kya lekar aayega. Lekin ek cheez zaroor jaanta hoon—

Aap meri life ki un sabse khoobsurat cheezon mein se hain jinke liye main hamesha grateful rahunga.

Aaj aapka birthday hai, isliye meri wish bas itni si hai:

Aap hamesha khush rahen.
Aapki smile kabhi kam na ho.
Aapke saare dreams poore hon.
Aur aapko zindagi mein woh saari khushiyan milen jo aap deserve karti hain.

Aur agar meri koi selfish wish ho...

Toh bas itni ki aapki zindagi ke bahut saare birthdays mein main aapke saath rahun, aapko sabse pehle wish karun, aur aap mujhe waise hi Medhak aur Lambu bolkar pareshaan karti rahen. 😂❤️

Happy Birthday, Sakshu.

Thank you for being you.
Thank you for being my peace.
Thank you for making ordinary moments feel special.

I love you. ❤️

Forever yours,
Your Medhak 🐸❤️
— Avi
